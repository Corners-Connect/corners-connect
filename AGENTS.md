# Repository agent guidance

## Open Corners City on localhost

When asked to open, run or show Corners City locally, read
`corners-city/board/CORNERS-CITY-LOCALHOST.md` and follow its launch workflow.
This is the single startup runbook for both Codex and Claude Code.
The city workspace is `corners-city/`; read its `CLAUDE.md` for local rules.
The full builder prompt is for feature work, and is not needed just to open it.

Apply this guidance to Corners City. Preserve the separate product in `app/`,
the website and the team's documents. A local launch does not authorize edits,
uploads, deployment, permission changes or messages to other people.
