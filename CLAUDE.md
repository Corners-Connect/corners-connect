# Corners City local launch

When asked to open, run or show Corners City on localhost, use the startup
runbook below. The city workspace is `corners-city/`; read
`corners-city/CLAUDE.md` for its local rules. The full builder prompt is only
needed for feature work. Preserve the separate app, website and team documents.
A local launch does not authorize uploads, deployment or permission changes.
