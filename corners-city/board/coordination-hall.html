01 · ROUTING RULESSOURCE: AUTOMATION/ROUTING.MD

Six rules, written once

One owner, one name: an alert never goes to two people or to a team. Alerts carry a lead id and a kind, never an address or a name.

R15 min

Welcome email. A student signs up and agreed to be emailed, and gets their first-week checklist. Owner of the rule: Audience.

R25 min

Partner alert. A partner writes (international office, provider, ESN section, residence). One named person is told. Owner of the rule: Audience.

R3 · R424 · 48 h

The day limit. No reply logged after 24 hours: the owner is reminded. After 48 hours: Operations is told.

  • R5 · No rule matches, or the AI enrich step marked the row wrong. It goes on the weekly review list. Nobody outside the twelve is contacted.Weekly
  • R6 · Any step fails twice after retrying. Operations gets the scenario, step, run id and error text.15 min

Who the owners are is the company's decision and is not filled in yet (routing.md section 2).

02 · INCIDENT CLINICFAILURE, IMPACT, RECOVERY, LESSON

When it breaks, nobody who signed up is left waiting

The error route retries twice, five minutes apart, then alerts Operations. The failed lead stays in the table, marked, so the weekly review finds it. Failures are logged in the playbook, one row each.

  • Forced failure, tested in test mode only: the email step is switched off and one invented sign-up is added.Not run
  • Pass mark, set before the run: 1 of 1 forced failure reported to Operations within 15 minutes, with a run id.Not run
  • Incident log: no incidents yet. A failure is written here after it happens, never before.Empty

Nothing on this page has been run. The numbers above are pass marks, not results. Every figure on the finished page will be a count, with its source and date, because the lists are under 30 records.
