# Corners City assets

Three.js r178 is a matched pair (`three.module.min.js` and `three.core.min.js`), copied from the instructor’s existing generic drawing engine. Its MIT license is in `LICENSE`. Do not replace only one module.

Instrument Serif and Space Grotesk are local font assets; their SIL Open Font Licenses are included. The Corners eye mark is adapted from the course identity favicon, with its SVG namespace supplied for embedding.

Building and robot geometry in `../city-models.js` is a clean adaptation of the generic `Freedom/twin-mundo/js/mapa.js` drawing functions. No business state, transports, people, roles, client cases, credentials or original interior data is included. The original project was read-only throughout.
