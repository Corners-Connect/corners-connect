TODAY’S HACKATHON

A place to build
your company.

Corners City makes your existing workspace tangible. Enter through a building, find an open plot, and make one useful thing work. Your team decides what to build and how the company runs.

Read the city.

Buildings are entries. Product, Audience and Operations open their existing company-board screens. Their names follow the supplied board template, which still uses sample data. They do not confirm current memberships or a fresh organizational decision.

The seven product-section buildings open their existing published app links in a new tab and display the board actions referenced in reviewed source data. These are entries into the product; no new work record is created by opening them.

Robots are visual demonstrations. Five blue characters move along a street. They do not execute tasks, call AI models or represent people. You can pause them.

Open plots are possibilities. Select the remaining open plot, choose an invented name and a shape, and build a visual shell. A workshop, studio or tower is a shape; students decide its purpose. Creating a shell does not create a task or finish a working tool.

Learning Lab.

The library on plot-08 is an instructor contribution: enter Learning Lab to prepare four official deliveries and connect all 25 syllabus topics to useful company pieces. Preparation steps are proposals; purpose, people and decisions stay yours. Copy/download briefs, review evidence and consult the course rules/source notes. It records no progress or grades.

On mobile, use Choose a place: its compact list opens the same details and actions for all eleven buildings and the remaining open plot. Floating map labels are hidden at this size so they cannot overlap the touch controls.

What works today.

Isometric 3D, clickable and keyboard-accessible place buttons, department links, drag to pan, zoom, Fit city, animated robots, a plot builder, browser-local saving and JSON export. There is no backend, login, AI execution or real-time collaboration.

Two different kinds of saving.

A browser draft stays in this browser at this exact host and port. Reloading normally keeps it. Another browser or laptop will not see it. If saving is blocked, the page tells you to export before closing. “Export city draft” downloads a JSON proposal.

A team source change is reviewed code. Give an exported draft to your agent, review it, then put accepted visual additions in city-data.js. Share changed files through the team's agreed method. Exporting does not merge code, publish a site or synchronize anybody’s draft.

Start on your own laptop.

With local Codex (desktop/CLI) or Claude Code, open the repository folder and ask: Open Corners City on localhost and leave the server running. Both agents use the startup runbook. The agent needs files and a terminal on your laptop; a remote sandbox has its own localhost. The full builder prompt is for feature work.

Clone or download the team repository, extract it, and open corners-city/. The standalone kit ZIP instead opens as Corners-City-GitHub/. In both cases the workspace is the folder containing start.py. Use a current browser with WebGL 2. Check for Python before installing it; use a supported stable Python 3.11–3.14 for a fresh setup. No npm build, database, hosting account or model key is needed. Fonts and Three.js r178 are already local.

Inside the city workspace (where start.py lives), run python3 start.py on Mac or py -3 start.py on Windows. If only python exists on Windows, use python start.py. Or double-click start.command / start.bat. Open the printed URL and keep the terminal open. Ctrl+C stops it. A localhost URL always belongs to the laptop where it is opened.

Build one thing that works.

Choose a new building shape, one student-defined place, a better interaction, or a small functioning tool inside an existing department. Define the demonstration first. Give your agent the full prompt and your local workspace. Keep company commitments in the existing board, and decisions in the Manual/Registry. No parallel system and no personal data.

Run python3 board/check.py on Mac or py -3 board/check.py on Windows, then test your actual feature in the browser. The checker covers board data; it cannot verify the 3D view. Review desktop/mobile, links, build/reload/export, keyboard controls and the chocolate/blue appearance.
