What is Brand House?

Brand House is the team's guide for creating, reviewing, and using AI-generated images for Corners. Its purpose is to help different people produce images that feel like one consistent brand.

The Corners look in six rules

  • A real moment: an ordinary scene in a real place at a real time; nobody posing for the camera.
  • Photograph, not illustration: a realistic documentary photo with warm natural light and natural colours.
  • About 20 years old: young adults dressed for the situation, never recognisable people.
  • Nothing written: no readable text, signs, prices or logos, on walls, clothes or shoes.
  • Words: a place, a time, a price. Never "community" or "vibes".
  • Marked as AI: every image says which tool and prompt made it.

Full rules, with the mistakes our first tests found, are in the master prompt, the step-by-step manual is the playbook, and the images the look is compared with are in the references folder.

1. Create an image

Start with the team's master prompt. Describe the scene, people, location, lighting, and mood. Keep images realistic and avoid logos, writing, and recognizable people.

Read the master prompt

View the saved ComfyUI flow. The master prompt is maintained in the GitHub project. Use the repository copy for the latest version.

2. Review before using

Check every generated image for:

  • Realistic people, hands, and faces
  • Accurate architecture and location
  • No accidental text, logos, or watermarks
  • Consistency with the Corners visual style
  • Appropriate usage rights and AI disclosure

Images are not approved automatically. A second person must review them first.

3. Choose the right format

  • Instagram feed: 1080 × 1350 (4:5)
  • Stories, Reels, TikTok: 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
  • Website landscape: 16:9 when appropriate

Keep the original image. Crop without stretching, preserve important subjects, and check quality after resizing or upscaling.

Generate → Review → Correct → Approve → Resize → Document → Publish.
