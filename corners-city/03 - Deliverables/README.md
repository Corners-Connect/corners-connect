## Three things about the dates

**They are real and there is no re-submission.** The instructor is the client, and
you deliver on the day you agreed. What is unlimited is showing him the work
*before* the date — as often as you want, for as long as you want, and he will
tell you whether it meets the standard.

**Every standard is published before you start.** If you do not know what "done"
means for one of these, that is a question to ask now, not a thing to discover in
the week of.

**One folder per deliverable**, created when you start it, not when you finish it.
A folder that appears the night before is a folder with one person's work in it.
