## Review of comfy-test-01.png — Jenna, 23 Oct 2026

Two students are shown with a paper map and phone. The image has natural colours and looks like a realistic photograph of Bilbao. However, the bridge does not accurately match the Zubizuri, the students appear younger than 22, and the map contains text-like details. Keep as a test, not approved.

## Review of ref-02-bilbao-bench-zubizuri.webp — Jenna, 25 Oct 2026

**Result: approved as a brand reference** (not a blanket approval for every commercial publication). Two students are seated by the Bilbao river, one reading and one eating. The Zubizuri bridge is recognizable; natural afternoon lighting and colours give the image a candid documentary look. No obvious logos, watermarks or legible unwanted signage. The book has printed-looking pages, but no clearly legible text at normal viewing size. The scene is consistent with the master prompt. Check rights and disclosure for each published use.

## Review of ref-03-club-dance-floor.webp — Jenna, 25 Oct 2026

**Result: approved as a brand reference** (not a blanket approval for every commercial publication). Three prominent young adults dance in a crowded indoor venue. The warm low light, natural clothing colours and spontaneous expressions resemble a candid photograph rather than an advertisement. The background crowd is appropriate to the club scene; no obvious readable text, logos or watermarks. The location is generic rather than identifiably Bilbao, which is acceptable for a general nightlife reference. Check rights and disclosure for each published use.
