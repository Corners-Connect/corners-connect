# Playbook: Publishing Gate (Building A13)

Owner: Pair 6 (Jose & Rylan)
Target Wave: January Intake

## The Safe Automation Principle
AI tools (LLMs) may draft content, but absolutely nothing is published automatically. Every outbound email and social post must pass a human verification gate before scheduling.

## The Review Checklist

A second person (not the creator) must verify these three gates before giving the "Owner's Yes":

### 1. The 70 / 25 / 5 Content Ratio
Check which bucket this post falls into:
- [ ] 70% Product: Showing Corners working in Spain (maps, features, real utility).
- [ ] 25% The Idea: Educational context for incoming students (visas, transit, housing advice).
- [ ] 5% The Twelve of Us: Behind-the-scenes building updates / team transparency.

### 2. Voice & Claims Standards
- [ ] Concrete Facts: Must contain a specific place, a specific time, and a price (if relevant).
- [ ] Zero Banned Buzzwords: Strictly NO "community", NO "vibes", NO empty marketing hype.
- [ ] Fact Verification: Dates, links, cohort codes, and prices double-checked.

### 3. Destination & Sign-off
- [ ] Channel confirmed (Instagram, TikTok, Arrival Group, or Email).
- [ ] Named person's approval recorded in the PR/task tracker.
