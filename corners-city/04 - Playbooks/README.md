# Playbooks

One per live competence. **The owner writes it; the apprentice produces the
deliverable from it.** That pairing is the whole mechanism by which eleven people
end the term able to do 21 things, instead of three people ending it able to do
everything.

A playbook is not documentation. It is written to be **used once, by somebody
under time pressure, who has not done this before**. Test: hand it to your
apprentice and leave the room. If they have to come and find you, it is not
finished.

Copy `TEMPLATE.md` and name it after the competence: `C-11-seo-brief.md`.


The Corners City builder guide and copy-ready agent prompt live with the executable city: [`../board/CORNERS-CITY-START.md`](../board/CORNERS-CITY-START.md) and [`../board/CORNERS-CITY-BUILDER-PROMPT.md`](../board/CORNERS-CITY-BUILDER-PROMPT.md).
