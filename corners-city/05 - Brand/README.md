# Brand

The brand book, the assets, and the rules for using them.

A brand book is not a logo. It is the document that lets somebody who was not in
the room produce something that looks like it came from this company. Five pages
is enough: **the mark** and its clear space, **the colours** with their hex
values, **the type** and its sizes, **the voice** with real examples of what this
company would and would not say, and **what not to do**, drawn.

That last page saves more work than the other four together.

## What goes in this folder

- `BRAND.md` — the book itself
- `logo/` — the mark, in vector and in raster, in every version that gets used
- `assets/` — anything else that has to look consistent

**Name the files so somebody can find them in December without asking.**
`logo-white-on-dark.svg`, not `logo-final-v3-REAL.svg`.
