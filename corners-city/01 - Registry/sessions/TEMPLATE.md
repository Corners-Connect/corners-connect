## The numbers

**They are not written here.** They are calculated by the board, from the
commitments and the hours you have already entered — because a number somebody
copies by hand on a Thursday afternoon is a number that stops being copied in
October.

Open the board's **Today** screen and read the four out loud:

| | |
|---|---|
| Commitments kept / made | delivery, and the half you can still act on |
| People who moved up a level | quality |
| What went out into the world | growth — published, sent, or used by somebody outside this room. *"Almost finished" is not an entry* |
| Different people who shipped | the one that warns first, and it is a count of people, never a mood |

**None of them ever touches anyone's grade.** They are yours, for you, and for
what you tell the room on 15 December.
