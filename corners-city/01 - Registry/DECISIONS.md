# Decisions

> If a decision is not written here, it was not taken. That is not a figure of
> speech: in three weeks nobody will remember it the same way, and the argument
> happens again with worse tempers.

Write the losing argument down too. It is the only way to find out, in December,
whether the person who lost was right.

## How a decision gets taken here

Whatever the Manual says. Until the Manual says otherwise, the minimum is:

1. The proposal fits in one sentence.
2. It is **discussed in the open and voted in secret** — so that a yes is an
   agreement and not a courtesy.
3. Nothing is decided without a name and a date on it.
4. It is written here: the count, and the best argument that lost. **Never who
   voted which way.**
5. What a vote does not settle is settled by whoever does the work.
