# 3 things nobody tells you about your first week abroad

**Hook:** "Day 3 in a new city and I still had no idea where to buy groceries."

**Body:**
1. Everyone is as lost as you. Talk to the person next to you at orientation.
2. Do the boring admin first: transport card, bank, phone plan.
3. Ask students who already live there. Their tips beat any guide.
