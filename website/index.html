04 — OUR DATA

We label what we know from what we guessed.

Every row carries a chip. Tap it and you get the source, the date, and what the number doesn't tell you.

Sourced

Real figures

Rents from idealista Q2–Q3 2026. Meals and safety from Numbeo. Climate from AEMET and IPMA 1991–2020 normals. English from the EF index 2025. Erasmus intake from European Commission microdata. Transport prices from the operators themselves.

Our rating

Judgement calls

Housing availability, transit quality, nightlife and outdoors are our ratings — informed, but not measured. Part-time work rules are indicative and need checking with a consulate.

Demo data

Placeholders

Student tip counts, example posts, listings and mock-up prices. Labelled everywhere they appear, and replaced before anything ships.

06 — FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is the housing section real?

Not yet. The portals shown are services students already use, but we have no partnership with them, and "Corners Verified" describes a process we would have to staff. It's a design mock-up until a city goes live.

Where do the numbers come from?

idealista for rents, Numbeo for meals and safety, AEMET and IPMA for climate, the EF index for English, European Commission microdata for Erasmus intake, and the transport operators for student passes. Anything that's our own judgement is labelled as our rating.

What does it cost?

Free for students. Universities and programmes pay for their programme page and the fortnightly pulse.

Why only Bilbao?

Because we're students in Bilbao, and a city app is worth nothing if the recommendations are wrong. Better right in one city than vague in ten.

Is Nearby anonymous?

To other students, yes. Moderators can always see who posted, which is what makes the rules hold. Posts that name or target a person are removed.
